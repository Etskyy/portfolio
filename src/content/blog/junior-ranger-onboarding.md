Spent this week on the first-run onboarding flow for Junior Ranger AI. The brief is a young
audience, so the goal was to get from app open to "doing something" in under three taps,
with no text-heavy explanation screens up front.

Landed on a short three-screen sequence that leans on illustration and a single tap-to-continue
gesture rather than a "next" button, then drops straight into a guided first activity instead
of a settings or profile step. Presenting this to the group later in the week.
